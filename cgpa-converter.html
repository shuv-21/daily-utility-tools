Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I convert CBSE CGPA to percentage?

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) in India uses a standard multiplier of 9.5. To find your percentage, simply multiply your total CGPA by 9.5. For example, if your CGPA is 8.0, your percentage is 8.0 x 9.5 = 76%.

Why is the multiplier customizable?

While CBSE schools use 9.5, many engineering colleges, state boards, and international universities use different conversion formulas. Some universities use a straight 10.0 multiplier (where 8.5 CGPA = 85%), while others use custom formulas (like subtracting 0.75 and multiplying by 10). We built the multiplier box so you can adjust the tool to fit your specific university's guidelines.

Is CGPA out of 4.0 or 10.0?

In India, the most common grading scale is out of 10.0. However, universities in the United States and Canada typically use a 4.0 scale. This calculator is optimized for the Indian 10.0 grading system.
