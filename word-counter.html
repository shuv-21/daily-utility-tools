Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my text saved or stored anywhere?

No, your privacy is strictly protected. This Word Counter operates entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your text is never sent over the internet or saved to any servers. It is 100% private and secure.

How is the Estimated Reading Time calculated?

The estimated reading time is based on the average adult reading speed, which is roughly 200 to 250 words per minute. Our tool takes your total word count and divides it to give you a highly accurate estimate of how long it will take someone to read your content.

Why use a character counter?

Many platforms have strict character limits. For example, X (formerly Twitter) allows 280 characters, while SMS text messages are usually limited to 160 characters. SEO meta descriptions are best kept under 160 characters as well. Our real-time character counter helps you stay perfectly within those limits without having to guess.
