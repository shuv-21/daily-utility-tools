Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the EMI calculation work?

The mathematical formula used to calculate your EMI is: E = P x R x (1+R)^N / [(1+R)^N-1].

Where E is the EMI, P is the Principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12), and N is the loan duration in months. Our tool processes this complex formula instantly within your browser.

Is this accurate for Indian Banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI)?

Yes. Indian banks use the standard reducing balance method to calculate EMIs, which is exactly the algorithm this calculator uses. Simply enter the interest rate provided by your bank to get your exact monthly payment.

How can I reduce my Total Interest payable?

You can reduce your total interest burden by either securing a lower interest rate, paying a higher down payment (reducing your principal amount), or opting for a shorter loan tenure. Keep in mind that a shorter loan tenure will increase your monthly EMI, but significantly decrease the interest paid over time.
