Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do these QR codes expire?

No, the QR codes generated on this website are static and will never expire. Because the data is encoded directly into the pattern of the image itself, the code will work forever as long as the underlying link or text remains active.

Is this tool safe and private?

Absolutely. We use a client-side JavaScript library to generate your QR code directly within your browser. This means the links or text you type are never sent to our servers or stored in any database. Your information remains 100% private.

Why is the background of the QR code white?

While our website uses a modern dark theme, smartphone cameras rely on high contrast to properly read the data in a QR code. The black squares must be placed on a solid white background to ensure fast and accurate scanning across all devices.
